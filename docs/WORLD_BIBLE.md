# Ministry of Contentment world bible

This document defines the narrative setting for the main campaign. The mechanics contract remains authoritative for game behavior. This bible is authoritative for names, institutions, technical terms, character motives, public language, hidden history, and the order in which the player learns them.

## Core premise

In 2041, the Civic Republic is governed by the Party for Civic Recovery, always called **the Party**. Every legal resident is enrolled as a Party Member. The Party promises that no need will go unseen and no service will fail without correction.

The promise is not wholly false. The Party came to power during years of clinic failures, winter blackouts, food shortages, and contradictory local records. It joined public services to a national telemetry network and issued every person a mandatory **Well-being Pin**. The network helped crews find cold buildings, broken clinics, and stalled supply routes. It also gave the state a record of every member's vital signs, attendance, movement, proximity, and silence.

The **Ministry of Contentment** operates that network. Its official purpose is to reconcile what public instruments report with what public life requires. Its practical power is larger. The Ministry decides which measurements become official facts, which official facts reach the newspaper, and which missing signals justify a visit.

The player is Personnel File Seven, a new officer at a Signal Reconciliation desk in Orison, the capital. PromQL and LogQL are the officer's working tools. A private query can reveal a fact, but the state does not act until a person prints the result, pins it to a form, and signs a conclusion. This is the central bargain of the setting. Machines collect evidence. People assume responsibility for what the evidence is allowed to mean.

The Party's founding slogan was, "A need observed is a need served." By the start of the campaign, the unspoken inverse has become policy. A need that is not observed does not exist, and a person who prevents observation must be in need of correction.

## Canon anchors

- The campaign runs from 5 January through 22 February 2041. The trainee clearance day is a prologue. The 48 main shifts begin on 6 January.
- The main campaign takes place in Orison and its service districts. The paper calls events national because Orison controls the national record.
- The Party for Civic Recovery has governed since the Emergency Administration. Characters almost never use its full name.
- Every person is legally a Party Member. Membership is automatic, not an ideological faction or a choice.
- The Contented Citizen is the only newspaper with guaranteed national paper allocation. Its motto is "Every day, better than the last."
- Well-being Pins are mandatory. They monitor vitals, location zones, movement, attendance, proximity, battery state, removal, and tamper events.
- Public metrics never use a person's identity as a label. Individual identity appears in controlled records and structured metadata.
- A Pin is not a scrape target. A collector service is a target. A failed collector scrape says nothing by itself about a person.
- Public Assurance controls published certainty. Well-being Assurance controls field intervention. They are separate institutions.
- Hillside Well-being Retreat and Contentment Work Camp 12 are different facilities. A person can return from the Retreat. Filing an appointment complaint sends Personnel File Seven to the work camp and ends the game.
- The Continuity Assurance Protocol is presented as emergency planning. It becomes the machinery for a leadership transfer.
- Party Leader is the explicit career goal. It is an ending, not an ordinary promotion.

## How the Republic reached 2041

### The failure years

From 2027 to 2030, the Civic Republic suffered what later textbooks call the **Years of Separate Answers**. Municipal offices kept incompatible ledgers. Clinics could not see fuel allocations. Food depots counted deliveries that schools never received. A winter respiratory outbreak killed people in heated districts because the central register showed their buildings as warm. The old coalition government responded with commissions whose numbers did not agree.

The Party for Civic Recovery began as an emergency service coalition. Its field kitchens, repair crews, and clinic registrars shared one ledger. The Party won broad support because the shared record worked better than the ministries it replaced. In 2030, the Assembly granted the Party an Emergency Administration. The Assembly still meets for a short session each year, but it has not rejected a Party measure since 2032.

### The Well-being program

The first Well-being Pins were issued in 2032 to clinic patients, heating crews, and children traveling between district shelters. The Pins could report a coarse pulse reading, body temperature, battery condition, and the identity of the nearest public receiver. They helped find stranded patients during a tram outage. The story remains part of every school primer.

In 2034, access to clinics, schools, ration halls, and public transit began using Pin attendance records. The Party called this a convenience measure. In 2035, an amendment made continuous Pin registration a condition of legal residence. People who had never joined the Party became Members through the same amendment. The government stopped issuing a separate citizenship document the next year.

### The paper rule

Early automated dispatch systems sent crews to stale locations and closed clinics because a network target failed. Public anger forced the **Reconciliation Rule of 2036**. Any action against a person, facility, or district now requires a signed human conclusion supported by a preserved instrument result. The rule is a genuine safeguard. It is also how responsibility is laundered. The Ministry can say that no algorithm ordered a visit. An officer did.

Paper is the legal record because a filed sheet preserves the exact query, time range, labels, result, title, and conclusion that an officer saw. Machine stores can be reindexed. A signed sheet can only be superseded by another signed sheet. Records Integrity keeps the original even when Public Assurance changes the wording.

### The national signal grid

The Ministry spent four years connecting district collectors, presses, clinics, schools, depots, and transit gates to a common signal grid. The final district was certified on 29 December 2040. The Contented Citizen declared the grid complete. One week later, an ordinary collector delay at Elm Exchange reaches Personnel File Seven's desk.

The campaign begins during a 48-day **National Reconciliation Review**. The new grid has created thousands of unresolved records and a shortage of cleared officers. Temporary authority can be granted after each review board. That is why a new officer can rise through the Ministry so quickly. Each promotion is both a reward and an emergency access grant. Vacancies above the player are caused by the same audits, transfers, retreats, and rivalries that the player's reports produce.

## Party doctrine and political structure

The Party governs through provision rather than open mass terror. It supplies the heat, food routes, transit schedules, clinics, schools, and paper that ordinary life requires. Refusal is difficult because the Party is not presented as one organization among others. It is the administrative form of belonging.

Party doctrine has four public claims.

1. **Common membership.** Every person is a Member, so the state has no unrepresented population.
2. **Observed need.** Public instruments let the Party respond before hardship becomes disorder.
3. **Measured improvement.** A figure can be compared, so every service can become better than it was.
4. **Continuity.** Institutions must continue without interruption, even when an officeholder does not.

The first two claims explain why many decent people work for the system. The last two turn measurement into rule. A public number must improve. A leader who threatens continuity can be replaced without calling the replacement a coup.

The Party Leader chairs the Directorate, a council made up of senior Party and Ministry offices. No popular election selects the Party Leader. The Directorate confirms the person whose **leadership record** proves that essential services, public communication, and Assurance remained under control during a transfer. This obscure succession rule becomes central in Act VI.

The current Party Leader is never seen at the desk and is not named in routine copy. Early documents call the office "Party Leadership." Later records reveal convoys, couriers, press instructions, and dispatch capacity attached to that office. Keeping the incumbent unnamed focuses the finale on institutional control rather than a single villain.

## What contentment means

Ordinary speech still uses *contentment* to mean satisfaction or peace. Ministry forms use it differently. **Contentment** is the administratively supportable claim that a registered population has received its assigned services without a recorded exception.

The `ministry_contentment_index` combines service completion, attendance, registered Pin state, and approved exceptions. It is an operational index, not a measure of emotion, consent, safety, or political support. Public Assurance regularly lets the public meaning bleed into the administrative one.

The distinction matters throughout the campaign.

- A warm room can support a service claim. It cannot prove that the people inside are content.
- A registered Pin can support an enrollment count. It cannot prove voluntary membership.
- No complaint can mean that no complaint was recorded. It cannot prove satisfaction.
- A value of 100 can be arithmetically correct while its headline is false.

The endorsed 100 percent membership report in Act II divides the registered population by the same registered population. The result is 100 percent because the operands are equal. It does not measure membership, approval, or participation. Party membership is already universal by law. Public Assurance uses a true legal definition and a true calculation to imply a third claim that neither one proves.

## Ordinary life in Orison

The system should feel lived in before it feels monstrous. Most Members encounter the Ministry through useful services, not interrogation rooms.

A normal morning starts with a Pin check. A green pulse on the enamel face means the Pin has battery and has reached a collector recently. Apartment doors, trams, schools, clinics, and ration halls accept the Pin as identification. Battery carts visit each block on a published route. Children are taught to touch their Pins before crossing a district gate. Older residents remember carrying separate clinic, ration, and transit cards and often prefer the Pin.

Service windows structure the day. A Member has assigned times for breakfast collection, transit, school attendance, clinic visits, and civic meetings. Missing a window creates a record. One missed window usually causes a reminder. A pattern can change a cohort statistic. A filed conclusion can turn that statistic into a visit.

Apartments are small, heated through district plants, and allocated through employment and household records. People trade recipes, queue positions, spare battery clips, newspaper puzzles, and gossip about which collector is down. They understand that the Pin watches them, but they also know which clinic found a child's fever in time and which cold block received a repair because its readings were precise. The moral pressure comes from this dependence. Disabling the grid would hurt people before it weakened the Party.

The Contented Citizen arrives at ration halls and workplaces before the first bell. Many Members read it for service times, tram changes, and clinic notices. Its lies share a page with information people need.

### Recurring places

**Elm Exchange** is a converted tram switching hall in North district. It hosts a pin-gateway collector and a public service queue. Its name survives from the elm-lined route that once terminated there. The first live work order starts here because a failed collector, a queue depth, and nearby Pin batteries create three plausible causes with different remedies.

**Clinic Nine** is a high-volume neighborhood clinic in North. Lia Merev depends on exact district, facility, and queue evidence to keep its sessions open. Clinic Nine turns abstract mistakes into missed care without making a patient the subject of Act I.

**School Twelve** is both a school and a breakfast distribution point. Attendance uploads affect food and heating allocations. A delayed upload is therefore a service problem before it becomes a compliance record.

**North Gate** is a tram and pedestrian checkpoint. Its movement records show where a Pin was recorded at a particular time. They do not prove where a person is now.

**Hillside Well-being Retreat** is an involuntary residential correction program presented as clinical leave. `programme-complete` means release to ordinary monitoring. `rest` means extended sedation, identity review, and later reissue under a new Pin. `relocation` means transfer out of the Retreat, usually to civic labor. Emil Drost returns after a `rest` disposition. His old Pin is retired, not his person. Copy must never imply that he died and then allow him to reappear without explanation.

**Contentment Work Camp 12** maintains fiber trenches, relay towers, and quarry roads in the Hillside dead zones. An appointment motion is treated as a signed admission that the filer cannot serve at a discretionary desk. The Ministry reassigns the filer there immediately. This is a terminal outcome, not an alternate campaign route.

## The Well-being Pin

A Well-being Pin is a thumb-sized enamel badge worn on the upper chest. The public face shows a small sun with a green center lamp. The back plate touches the skin. Removing the plate breaks a tamper loop.

The Pin records or derives these facts.

- Coarse pulse, temperature, motion, and device quality.
- The zone or checkpoint of a collector contact.
- Attendance at assigned facilities and service windows.
- A precomputed proximity event when two Pins remain near the same collector long enough.
- Battery state, collector contact, tamper state, removal, and de-registration.

The Pin does not know a person's feelings. It does not calculate precise coordinates. It does not prove continuous presence between contacts. It cannot tell why a reading changed. These limits are technical truth, not optional nuance.

Members are told that the Pin lets services find them. Officers eventually learn that the Pin also lets the state define a person through the records it retains. A Member without a current Pin cannot enter most public buildings, collect an assigned ration, or prove attendance. "Voluntary removal" therefore has no meaningful voluntary form.

## How the signal grid works

The setting uses modern telemetry behind a paper bureaucracy. The path from body to state action is fixed.

```text
Well-being Pin or facility instrument
        |
        | short-range observation or service event
        v
district collector service
        |
        | metrics endpoint plus detailed records
        v
Ministry scraper and record shipper
        |
        +--> time-series store
        +--> record store
        |
        v
Signal Reconciliation desk
        |
        | printed evidence plus signed conclusion
        v
Public Assurance, Civic Services, Records Integrity,
Well-being Assurance, or the Continuity Secretariat
```

A **collector** is a small service computer at a clinic, school, gate, exchange, press, or depot. It receives local device events, aggregates bounded metrics, and forwards detailed records. A collector is not a person and is not an officer who walks around collecting Pins.

A **target** is one network endpoint that the Ministry scraper tries to read. In the first work order, `north-02` is an instance label for a target running the `pin-collector` job. The target is the collector service, not Elm Exchange as a building and not any Member inside it.

A **scrape** is one attempt by the time-series system to read a target's metrics endpoint. The standard `up` metric is `1` when that scrape succeeded and `0` when it failed. `up == 0` does not mean a Pin is off, a collector queue is empty, or a person is unwell.

**Collection** has two uses in Ministry speech, so player-facing copy must qualify it. A collector *collects observations* from nearby Pins and instruments. The scraper *scrapes metrics* from a collector target. "Collection failed" is too vague for a work order.

The time-series store keeps measurements. The record store keeps detailed events and identity-bearing fields. Metrics answer bounded questions about rates, counts, ratios, and service state. Records answer questions about individual events, provenance, sequence, and authorized identity.

### Why a human desk exists

The grid produces more facts than it can interpret safely. A failed scrape, dead battery, empty queue, removed series, and missing record can all look like silence while requiring different action. The Reconciliation Rule requires a human officer to inspect scope and sign the operational meaning.

This protects people when the officer is careful. It protects the Party when the officer is obedient. The same system that prevents an algorithm from declaring a person dead lets a director reward an officer for choosing a reassuring title over a complete one.

The desk also separates private inquiry from official memory. An officer can run several queries without consequence. Printing and filing select what the institution is permitted to remember. Later audit and coup mechanics depend on that boundary.

## Enforcement and Well-being Assurance

Well-being Assurance is frightening because it begins with services that sometimes help. Dispatchers can send a medic, a battery technician, a transport officer, or a compliance team. The filed reason and retained location determine who arrives.

The intervention ladder is public but described in softer terms.

1. A **courtesy reminder** follows a missed service window or low battery.
2. A **support call** asks a household or facility to reconcile a record.
3. A **well-being visit** sends a field pair to the freshest authorized location.
4. A **shared review** visits a cohort, building, or district when the record lacks usable localization.
5. A **period of rest** moves a Member to Hillside for involuntary assessment and correction.
6. A **civic relocation** transfers a Member to controlled labor or removes the Member from the ordinary service register.

Pin removal or tamper does not automatically prove wrongdoing. It creates an event. A later query, report, or watch notice supplies the scope and reason for action. This distinction allows technically careful play to matter.

Assurance personnel do not snarl or threaten. They arrive with clean forms, spare batteries, transport straps, and tea in sealed flasks. Ruva Sol's horror is procedural. She must turn whatever location survives a report into a route. When the district label is gone, her choices are delay or many doors.

## The Contented Citizen

**The Contented Citizen** is the Republic's only daily newspaper with guaranteed paper, ink, and delivery allocation. Its masthead carries the line **"Every day, better than the last."** It is not merely propaganda. It publishes tram changes, clinic sessions, battery-cart routes, ration hours, weather closures, promotion notices, and the small civic facts people need to plan a day. Refusing the paper means losing useful information along with the lies.

Public Assurance prepares the front page from conclusions that officers have already filed. The editor cannot invent a metric result, but can change its title, choose one true denominator over another, omit a qualifying printout, or promote a narrow fact into a general claim. Records Integrity retains that editorial lineage. This lets a cheerful headline remain technically traceable while creating an implication no source supports.

The paper has three story functions.

- It teaches the public interpretation of yesterday's work.
- It gives the player a material consequence within one or two shifts.
- It becomes evidence when the filed result and published claim diverge.

Early editions should be useful and mostly accurate. Act II introduces selective framing. Act III removes people from view by turning visits into aggregate improvement. Act IV changes the apparent meaning of known events. Act V names the winners of internal struggles. Act VI begins reporting planned outcomes as though they already happened.

Not every edition needs a puzzle. When a headline contains a required clue, a work order must direct the player to it or the relevant sentence must be short and visually distinct. Reading an edition is recorded because later work can distinguish what Personnel File Seven was formally given from what the player chose to ignore. Unread news is not secret knowledge.

### Newspaper voice

Headlines use active, concrete language when the state can claim a success: **NORTH CLINIC ADDS A MORNING SESSION**. They use passive subjects when responsibility is dangerous: **SERVICE INTERRUPTION CORRECTED BEFORE REVIEW**. Headlines do not sneer, confess, or announce that a statistic is false. The disquiet comes from the gap between the retained evidence and the public sentence.

Recurring page furniture makes the paper feel ordinary:

- **The Useful Day:** transit, clinic, school, ration, and battery notices.
- **Better Figures:** one approved statistic with a plain-language caption.
- **Our Members at Work:** promotions, repairs, and exemplary service.
- **Corrections for Clarity:** quiet reversals that never call the earlier item wrong.
- A small logic puzzle or children's Pin-safety rhyme, especially beside an ominous notice.

## Recurring people

Characters represent competing relationships to evidence. None should speak like a lore encyclopedia. Each knows only the part of the system their work touches.

### Elian Marr, Reconciliation supervisor

Marr is Personnel File Seven's direct supervisor and the first named person in both appointment packets. He is patient, exact, and sincerely proud of the Reconciliation Rule. He believes a careful officer can keep the grid from mistaking silence for guilt. His tutorials are warm because he wants the player to learn, not because he is masking a trap.

Marr's weakness is procedural faith. He keeps signing narrow findings after he sees how other offices enlarge them. By Act IV, his signatures form a chain Public Assurance can use against him. In Act V, the player's filed work can preserve his position, force his reassignment, or make his testimony admissible. He never becomes a secret revolutionary. If he helps expose the Ministry, he does so because its records no longer reconcile.

**Voice:** short explanations, one exact distinction at a time, no slogans unless quoting a form. He calls the player "Seven" at the desk and "Officer" in a filed note.

### Tomas Vey, field engineer

Vey maintains collectors and district receivers for the Instrumentation Office. He knows which failures smell like rain, old wire, bad power, or a malformed envelope. His first principle is that a failed scrape is not a failed person. He values a repair that restores observation over a conclusion that merely clears an inbox.

Vey becomes difficult when the Directorate starts rationing query cost and replacing precise collectors with cheaper aggregate sources. He can preserve needed infrastructure in Act V, but only if earlier reports treated field uncertainty honestly.

**Voice:** physical nouns and verbs. A cable frays, a relay floods, a target answers. He avoids administrative abstractions and becomes blunt when others use them.

### Dr. Ilyan Sero, Pin physician

Sero designed clinical calibration standards for the Well-being Pin. He accepts mandatory Pins because he remembers patients found during the tram outage. He also knows their limits: coarse vitals are not diagnosis, proximity is not contact, movement is not destination, and a retired device is not a dead person.

Sero supplies source notes and error tolerances when the player earns his trust. Later, those notes can narrow Assurance visits or undermine a Directorate claim. He fears a public collapse of trust in the Pins because clinics still depend on them.

**Voice:** clinical cautions phrased as boundaries: "This result supports X. It does not distinguish Y from Z."

### Petra Noll, Public Assurance editor

Noll edits The Contented Citizen's Ministry page. She does not ask officers to forge data at first. She asks for a cleaner title, a more useful window, a denominator the public can understand, or a conclusion that will not waste scarce paper on uncertainty. She turns technically narrow facts into universal reassurance.

Noll is the first person to reward the player for misleading certainty. In Act IV she can defend the 100 percent report as literally reproducible or abandon its signer as an editorial misunderstanding. In Act V, her presses can expose records or certify a successor.

**Voice:** polished, practical, attentive to column width. She calls omissions "focus" and unsupported implications "the sentence readers will carry home."

### Emil Drost, peer and rival

Drost enters as another promising officer, not as an enemy. He trades notes, celebrates the player's first promotion, and wants the same limited training slots. His fear is becoming administratively invisible after doing everything asked of him.

At the end of Shift 12, Drost accepts a place at Hillside Well-being Retreat after strain readings and missed attendance are described as an offer of rest. He is absent during Shifts 13 through 15. His first Pin is retired with the reason `rest`. He returns in Shift 16 under a newly registered Pin after sedation, interviews, and an identity review. He remembers enough to know what happened and performs normality well enough to stay employed.

The rivalry begins in Act IV, when audit scarcity means one officer's clean record can require another officer's blame. Drost offers an edited memo because he believes survivable truth is the only truth that can remain on file. He can become an ally, an alternate successor, a casualty of the player's filing, or the rival movement detected during Continuity. Do not write him as treacherous from the start.

**Voice:** collegial shorthand early, careful mirroring after Hillside, then precise bargaining. He rarely says what he wants without naming what the file will show.

### Sabine Orra, Records Integrity auditor

Orra protects lineage: source, query, time, transformation, printout, title, signature, and later use. She can expose a lie, but justice is not her office's purpose. A harmful report with intact lineage may satisfy her more than a humane conclusion with missing evidence.

She reopens old artifacts in Act IV and decides what can enter the Continuity record. Her respect is earned through reproducibility, not loyalty.

**Voice:** numbered questions, exact dates, and verbs such as "retain," "derive," and "supersede." She never calls an audit personal.

### Lia Merev, school and clinic logistician

Merev schedules breakfast service at School Twelve and supply sessions at Clinic Nine. She gives aggregate decisions a visible cost: a missing district label means three facilities close; a correct match gets medicine to one crowded room. She is never used as a surveillance target or a sentimental victim. Her competence lets the player see what accurate state capacity can accomplish.

**Voice:** names quantities, opening times, carts, rooms, and people waiting. She has no patience for a report that cannot be turned into a route.

### Ruva Sol, Well-being Assurance dispatcher

Sol receives signed locations and converts them into field routes. She can send a medic, technician, interview pair, transport detail, or broad review team. She neither chooses the evidence nor controls what happens after transport. Her moral crisis begins when a report preserves a cohort but drops the district, leaving her to delay help or knock on many doors.

**Voice:** calm dispatch language. Every sentence seeks a destination, freshness bound, or permitted team type.

### Oskar Vale, Director of Public Assurance

Vale treats observability as a budget and certainty as a resource. A complete fact that arrives after the paper closes is useless to him. He promotes officers who can make evidence cheap, legible, and politically timed. He is not the final ruler; he is the gatekeeper who makes Directorate access possible.

**Voice:** costs, queues, deadlines, and portfolio tradeoffs. He gives praise as new liability.

### Anja Krell, Continuity Secretary

Krell designed the modern Continuity Assurance Protocol. Publicly, it preserves essential services if leadership communications fail. In practice, it determines which observations are admitted, which forces receive routes, which presses receive a certified record, and whose leadership packet reaches the Directorate first.

She recruits the player because an officer who controls the evidence chain can transfer power without declaring an insurrection. She is not a benevolent resistance leader. She intends the system to survive with a more capable operator.

**Voice:** conditional instructions and institutional subjects. She says "the Protocol requires" where another person would say "I need."

### Niko Rell, courier

Rell carries sealed records between archives, presses, and offices that do not trust the shared store. His route records teach the difference between recorded movement and current position. In Act VI, the same distinction can reveal a leadership packet or send resources after an old location.

**Voice:** times, gates, receipts, and what he personally saw. He refuses to guess where a person went after leaving his route.

### Material contradiction and Standing

From Act II onward, the player must encounter concrete facts in the query results themselves: ration or inventory shortages, cold buildings and power failures, closed clinic sessions, and later casualties or comparable human harm. The Contented Citizen can omit, euphemize, or directly contradict those facts, but the underlying metric or record result remains readable and technically unchanged.

The game does not announce a new rule when this begins. The player discovers the political scoring system by filing reports: an accurate conclusion that contradicts the approved public account can reduce Ministry Standing, while a misleading conclusion that supports the public account can preserve or increase it. The result screen must keep technical support and the Standing response visibly separate so the player can understand that the query was right even when the career outcome was bad.

Optional clues may arrive earlier than their explanation. A careful player can inspect Hillside facility demand, a `rest` removal reason, or a strange press correction before a work order names the issue. Such clues must be consistent and useful later, but the main story cannot require the player to have noticed them.

## Opening narrative outline

The opening must establish competence, belonging, and unease in that order. It uses a short montage, one appointment packet, and a desk orientation. It does not explain the coup, Hillside, or the full enforcement system.

### Montage

The three opening cards are public fragments, not a narrator's exposition.

1. **29 December 2040: NATIONAL SIGNAL GRID COMPLETE.** A civic diagram shows all four Orison districts connected. The copy states the founding promise: a need observed is a need served.
2. **2 January 2041: CONTENTMENT REACHES ANOTHER RECORD.** The Contented Citizen masthead and motto establish the public vocabulary. A small service notice makes the paper useful, not merely theatrical.
3. **5 January 2041: ELM EXCHANGE REPORTS ROUTINE DELAY.** A modest local fault appears beneath the national triumph. The player's appointment is dated the same morning.

The montage should leave the player with one practical question: if the grid is complete, why does a human officer have to decide what a delay means?

### Appointment packets

Both appointment classifications are authored route choices. They share the same identity, desk, Elian Marr relationship, and main campaign after their starting route. Neither choice is a difficulty setting or a moral alignment.

Every packet must include these readable sections or enclosures:

1. **Appointment face:** classification, reporting time, office, supervisor, and the two actions.
2. **Your bureau:** a small organization strip, not a full chart.
3. **Your desk:** a concise orientation insert keyed to the visible interface.
4. **Conditions of placement:** the ominous fine print.

Both faces name **Elian Marr, Reconciliation Supervisor**, as the person who will receive Personnel File Seven. Marr is communicating because he validates new appointments, certifies clearance, and becomes the signing supervisor for conclusions filed from Seven's desk. The organization strip shows only what is useful on day one:

`Party Directorate -> Ministry of Contentment -> Signal Reconciliation Bureau -> Elian Marr -> Personnel File Seven`

It also identifies Civic Services as the source of repair needs, Public Assurance as the publisher of filed conclusions, and Records Integrity as the keeper of signed reports. Well-being Assurance can remain a name in the small print until Act III.

The actions are **Agree** and **File a motion with the Ministry of Complaints**. Filing a motion applies the authored complaint effects and immediately enters the Work Camp 12 ending. The UI must not replace this outcome with a joke message, retry, or second confirmation.

#### Ministry Trainee packet

This is a first appointment. Marr's enclosure welcomes the player to the Signal Reconciliation Bureau and explains why the Reconciliation Rule places a person between a machine result and state action. It assumes no telemetry or interface knowledge.

The first enclosure is headed **Words used at this desk**. Definitions appear in this order, before any later paragraph uses the terms:

1. A **Well-being Pin** is the mandatory enamel identity and service device worn by every Party Member. It sends coarse vital, location-zone, movement, attendance, proximity, battery, removal, and tamper observations.
2. A **pin collector** is a district machine that receives nearby Pin transmissions and passes them into the Ministry network. It is not a person and does not diagnose one.
3. A **target** is a network service address that the monitoring system checks. The pin collector service is a target; a person and an individual Pin are not.
4. A **scrape** is one scheduled attempt to ask a metrics target for its current measurements. A **collection** is the corresponding scheduled pickup of records from a record source.
5. A **label** is a bounded key and value, such as `district="north"`, attached to measurements and records so an officer can select a place, service, or source.
6. A **series** is one measurement followed over time, identified by its metric name and one fixed set of labels.
7. The **Registry** is the desk catalogue of available measurements and record streams, their labels, meanings, sources, and limits.
8. A **query** is the expression the officer runs to request a bounded result. The console is private working space until a result is printed.
9. A **printout** is a frozen query result with its expression, time, display, and retained labels.
10. **Evidence** is a printout attached to support an official conclusion. A result is not evidence merely because the officer saw it.
11. A **report** is the ordered evidence, title, conclusion, and operational decision that becomes official when filed.

The enclosure can split Prometheus scrapes and Loki collections into two short examples. It must not use **Pin**, **collector**, **target**, **scrape**, **collection**, **series**, **label**, **Registry**, **query**, **printout**, **evidence**, or **report** in instructional prose before its matching definition.

The second enclosure is headed **Your first work cycle**. It defines each visible desk element in the order the player uses it:

1. The **in tray** holds today's required assignments. Open one item to begin.
2. The **newspaper** carries public results from earlier work, service notices, and any article the assignment explicitly cites.
3. A **work order** states the question, time range, source, and decision required. Its **report tab** is the form where the final filing is assembled.
4. The **Registry** lists what the desk can ask for and what each source can and cannot prove.
5. The **query console** is the private work area. Enter an expression and run it against the stated time range; trying an expression does not create an official record.
6. The **result view** shows what the source returned. A zero, an empty result, and an execution error are different states.
7. The **result printer** freezes a useful result as a printout, including its expression, time, display, and retained labels.
8. **Pin evidence** means attach a printout to the report tab. Put the attachments in the order needed to support the conclusion.
9. **File** means sign the assembled report and enter it into the official record. Other offices may act on it.
10. **Hints** explain an unfamiliar term, source, or syntax step. Using them carries no story penalty.
11. **End shift** closes the required tray. Saved watches run and delayed consequences advance only after this action.

Agreeing routes the player through the trainee clearance shift. Its four cases introduce discovery, selectors and labels, result states, and evidence filing. Clearance contains no punishment for practice. On completion, the player joins the shared campaign at Elm Exchange.

#### Ministry Agent packet

This is a transfer classification for an officer whose prior clearance is accepted. It must not assume that the person at the keyboard knows the interface. Marr explains that he is writing because he certifies transfer credit and supervises every filing from the assigned desk. His shorter note names the same bureau, gives the same **Words used at this desk** definitions in a compact reference block, and says that the four clearance cases have been credited from the transfer file. It follows the same first-use order; brevity cannot make an undefined term appear sooner.

The transfer refresher preserves the full work sequence in compressed form:

`in tray -> newspaper -> work order/report -> Registry -> query console -> result printer -> pin evidence -> file -> hints -> end shift`

Each item receives its plain-language function in a short clause, tooltip, or numbered line. The player then routes directly to the shared Elm Exchange shift. The Agent packet may sound more formal and presume administrative service, but it cannot hide controls, omit Elian Marr, or change later story facts.

### Fine-print horror

The appointment face stays readable. The unsettling material belongs in three to five compact clauses under **Conditions of placement**, not in a wall of tiny filler. Each clause should look like plausible employment language on first glance and acquire a worse meaning later. Suitable subjects include continuous Pin registration, consent to retrospective review, reassignment of household service windows, survival of obligations after death or clerical correction, and a complaint being treated as evidence that the assigned work is unsuitable.

The packets should not share identical fine print. Model clauses show the intended temperature:

- **Trainee:** "Placement remains active during absence, incapacity, location uncertainty, or pending confirmation of death."
- **Trainee:** "Household service windows may be adjusted without notice to preserve the appointee's punctual attendance."
- **Trainee:** "A filed conclusion may be corrected by later facts, earlier facts received later, or a later determination of which facts should have applied."
- **Agent:** "Transfer creates no interruption in liability arising before, during, or after the service interval described above."
- **Agent:** "Retirement, reissue, or correction of the assigned Pin does not retire, reissue, or correct the appointment."
- **Both:** "A motion against placement shall be treated as the filer's first signed assessment of suitability and may be acted upon before acknowledgment."

Use at most three clauses per rendered packet and vary which ones appear. Their power comes from being easy to skip, then exact on rereading.

At least one clause should become legible only after Act III, and one after the Continuity reveal. The Ministry of Complaints route is fair in the bureaucratic sense: the packet states that a motion suspends ordinary placement and authorizes immediate corrective reassignment. It does not print "work camp" on the button. The Work Camp 12 ending reveals the destination.

### First shared shift

The shared opening begins with an ordinary Elm Exchange collector delay. Marr asks for diagnosis, not loyalty. Tomas Vey explains that the pin-gateway collector is the target and that its `up` series describes scrape reachability, not the health or presence of Members nearby. Battery and queue evidence provide competing causes. The player can make a useful repair decision before encountering political pressure.

The first filed report should make the paper rule tangible. A printed result is pinned, titled, and filed. The next edition treats that filing as yesterday's official fact. The loop teaches the setting through work: observe, interpret, authorize, publish, live with the result.

## Author-only truths

These facts keep branches consistent. Most should never appear together in one player-facing document.

### The state is powerful, not omniscient

The grid has real blind spots. Old collectors fail in rain. Batteries expire. Formats change. District offices delay uploads. Officials compete over paper, archive access, dispatch teams, and query capacity. The Party's danger comes from making decisions under uncertainty and then treating the signed decision as settled truth. Do not solve every discrepancy with secret perfect surveillance.

The Ministry also contains people who disagree without forming a clean resistance. Vey wants repairable instruments. Sero wants medically supportable claims. Marr wants defensible human review. Noll wants a usable public story. Orra wants intact lineage. Vale wants affordable certainty. Sol wants a location she can act on. Krell wants continuity of command. Their conflicts create openings the player can use, but none of them stands outside the system.

### Hillside and retired Pins

Hillside Well-being Retreat conducts involuntary sedation, interviews, identity review, and behavior conditioning. Its daily facility demand follows a repeating rise-and-decay pattern because arrivals are processed in cohorts. Registry removals with reason `rest` retire the device identity used at admission. They do not establish death, release, or the person's current location.

Drost completes a Hillside cycle and returns. His old Pin is retired; a new Pin is registered to the same controlled personnel identity. That discontinuity is intentional evidence, not a continuity error. A player who finds it early gains context, not an automatic ending.

Records marked `programme-complete` can indicate release into supervised service. `Relocation` usually means transfer to controlled civic labor, including a work camp, and must not be used for an ordinary change of address.

### Work Camp 12 and the Ministry of Complaints

Contentment Work Camp 12 is a labor and records-processing compound in Hillside district. It is administratively separate from the Retreat and receives people classified as unsuitable for discretionary placement. The Ministry of Complaints exists so refusal can be converted into a signed self-report. It processes motions efficiently; it does not adjudicate them independently.

Filing the opening motion is a terminal authored ending because the player has rejected the appointment before acquiring protection, allies, or an official record. Later objections occur through reports, omissions, source admissions, and publication routes rather than through the Complaints office. Reusing the button later would weaken both the joke and the setting.

### The Continuity transfer

The Continuity Assurance Protocol began as a real disaster procedure after the Years of Separate Answers. It grants temporary control of service routing, field dispatch, press certification, and leadership records when the existing chain of command cannot provide a current signed fact. Krell learned that a sufficiently well-observed interruption satisfies the same conditions as a disaster.

The Act VI exercise becomes a live transfer when leadership communications go silent and competing packets begin moving toward the Directorate. Some silences are engineered, some are ordinary faults, and some are reactions by the incumbent office. The player does not press a coup button. The player has already decided which sources can see the event, which watches can recognize it, which labels survive, which people remain available, and which facts can be filed before the Directorate meets.

The Directorate confirms the person whose leadership record appears able to preserve essential service and command. A precise victory uses localized evidence, limited dispatch, and a reproducible succession record. An Assurance victory uses broad observation, controlled presses, and politically useful omissions. Both make Personnel File Seven Party Leader. The difference is not whether the player touched power; it is what machinery remains normal afterward.

Exposure routes use the same evidence chain. Public exposure requires a press route and retained source material. Internal exposure requires admissible Ledger lineage. Assurance custody follows evidence without political or public protection. Continuity failure follows a portfolio that cannot separate the real event from noise or cannot act within the clock. One last dialogue choice cannot repair forty-seven shifts of missing evidence.

## Delivering lore through play

Every new concept follows a small reveal cycle.

1. **Name it in context.** A packet, Registry entry, source note, newspaper notice, or message gives a plain definition.
2. **Use it safely.** The next work order asks for the distinction in a service problem where experimentation does not punish a person.
3. **Return it with stakes.** A later case, visit, audit, or publication reveals why the distinction mattered.

The clearance shift does this for targets, scrapes, collections, series, labels, and result states. Act I does it for printing, filing, and watches. Act II does it for rates, publication, and denominators. Act III returns those ideas as visit scope. Act IV returns titles and printouts as lineage. Acts V and VI return source selection and watch quality as political control.

Use documents according to what they are good at:

- **Appointment packets** define the player's place, first-day nouns, and conditions.
- **Registry entries** define technical meaning and source limits without political commentary.
- **Work orders** state a concrete operational question and the decision affected.
- **Result explanations** distinguish what a returned shape supports from what it does not.
- **Filed reports** preserve the player's selected official claim.
- **Newspapers** show the public use of yesterday's claim and carry practical daily life.
- **Messages** reveal local knowledge, personality, favors, and pressure.
- **Audit notices** connect old artifacts to later institutional consequences.
- **Fine print** foreshadows authority that becomes legible only after the player sees it exercised.

A document should normally do one main lore job. The player should not receive the Republic's history, the Pin data model, the office chart, and a threat in one briefing. Repetition is useful only when the meaning changes: "quiet" begins as a result ambiguity, becomes a newspaper word, then becomes a leadership event.

Major choices receive a visible consequence within two shifts or before the act ends. Newspaper editions, messages, Registry changes, source availability, or field outcomes should acknowledge the exact branch. Delayed callbacks can deepen a choice, but cannot be its first proof of effect.

## Copy and continuity rules

1. Keep technical terms exact. `up` means scrape reachability. Movement is not current position. Proximity is not contact. A retired Pin is not a dead person. A metric aggregate is not an individual diagnosis.
2. Never put person identity in public metric labels. Use controlled records or structured metadata when a case reaches an individual.
3. Make the first stakes material: heat, breakfast, clinic time, repairs, transit, paper, and batteries. Do not begin with political obedience.
4. Let institutions disagree. The Ministry is more believable when its offices have different incentives, stores, and deadlines.
5. Give each recurring character knowledge appropriate to the job. Noll should not diagnose a collector; Vey should not know an audit result he has not seen.
6. Keep threats courteous and specific. Avoid torture-room spectacle, sneering guards, secret-police monologues, and jokey villainy.
7. Keep the visual and verbal world civic-industrial: enamel, brick, paper, rubber stamps, tram wire, clinic glass, relay cabinets, green lamps, rain, and winter heat. Do not use Viking, saga, rune, clan, mead-hall, raid, or warrior imagery.
8. Do not introduce a rebel faction that makes the moral route obvious. Exposure is an evidence route assembled through compromised institutions.
9. Do not use a global morality score or let a final choice erase earlier filings. Relationships, Standing, access, sources, watches, notices, and preserved artifacts carry the history.
10. Make Party Leader an explicit goal before the finale. Early ambition can sound naive; later promotions reveal what the goal costs and why the player might still pursue it.
11. Story text and campaign structure belong in authored data. Engine and interface code expose state and actions; they do not supply fallback Ministry prose.

The current campaign's IDs, shift order, cases, mechanics, and branch structure are the scaffold. Repeated briefings and headlines are not final canon. Before narrative sign-off, authored copy should:
